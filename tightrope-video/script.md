# High Wire — Script

Vertical video (9:16), ~30 seconds, one calm narrator. 79 words.

## 1. Narration (word for word)

> This is a single steel cable between two mountains, and someone is walking across it.
> The first trick is the long pole.
> Its weight sits far out on each side, so the walker tips more slowly.
> That slow tip gives the walker time to correct.
> Thin, soft soles let the feet feel the wire.
> And the eyes stay on the far end, not on the drop below.
> Small, steady fixes, one step at a time, all the way across.

## 2. Shot list

One continuous camera move. No cuts. The cable's long span and all labels are drawn by the animation.
